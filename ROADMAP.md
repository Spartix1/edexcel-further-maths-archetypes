# Master Syllabus Roadmap & Progress Checklist

This roadmap tracks all chapters across the four target Edexcel Further Mathematics modules.

---

## 📘 Core Pure Mathematics 1 (CP1)

- [x] **Chapter 1: Complex Numbers** — Basic arithmetic, Cartesian form, complex conjugates, solving quadratics/cubics/quartics.
- [x] **Chapter 2: Argand Diagrams** — Modulus-argument form, multiplying/dividing in polar form, basic loci ($|z - z_1| = r$, $|z - z_1| = |z - z_2|$).
- [x] **Chapter 3: Series** — Standard summations ($\sum r, \sum r^2, \sum r^3$), method of differences, finding general sum $S_n$.
- [x] **Chapter 4: Roots of Polynomials** — Vieta's formulas for quadratics, cubics, quartics; forming new equations with transformed roots ($w = 2\alpha - 1$).
- [x] **Chapter 5: Volumes of Revolution** — Rotation around $x$-axis and $y$-axis; compound shapes and hollow solids.
- [ ] **Chapter 6: Matrices** — $2\times2$ and $3\times3$ addition, multiplication, determinants, inverses; solving simultaneous equations; consistency and geometric arrangements of planes.
- [ ] **Chapter 7: Linear Transformations** — $2\times2$ and $3\times3$ transformation matrices; invariant points and invariant lines; area scale factor ($|\det M|$).
- [ ] **Chapter 8: Proof by Mathematical Induction** — Summation proofs, divisibility proofs, matrix power proofs, recurrence relation proofs.
- [ ] **Chapter 9: Vectors** — Equation of lines (vector, parametric, Cartesian); scalar (dot) product; angle between lines; shortest distance between points, lines, and skew lines.

---

## 📕 Core Pure Mathematics 2 (CP2)

- [ ] **Chapter 1: Complex Numbers (Advanced)** — de Moivre's theorem, Euler's relation ($e^{i\theta}$), $\cos(n\theta)$ and $\sin^n(\theta)$ expansions, $n$-th roots of unity, geometric loci and half-lines ($\arg(z-a) = \theta$).
- [ ] **Chapter 2: Series (Advanced)** — Maclaurin series expansions of standard functions, combining series, evaluation of limits using series.
- [ ] **Chapter 3: Methods in Calculus** — Leibniz's rule for differentiation of products, improper integrals, mean value of a function, integration using partial fractions.
- [ ] **Chapter 4: Polar Coordinates** — Sketching polar curves $r = f(\theta)$, tangents parallel and perpendicular to the initial line, enclosed area $\frac{1}{2}\int r^2 d\theta$.
- [ ] **Chapter 5: Hyperbolic Functions** — Definitions of $\sinh, \cosh, \tanh, \text{sech}, \text{cosech}, \text{coth}$; identities; inverse hyperbolic functions and logarithmic expressions; calculus of hyperbolic functions.
- [ ] **Chapter 6: Methods in Differential Equations** — First-order linear DEs using integrating factors ($I = e^{\int P dx}$); second-order linear homogeneous and non-homogeneous DEs (Auxiliary equation, CF + PI).
- [ ] **Chapter 7: Modelling with Differential Equations** — Damped harmonic motion, resonance, coupled first-order linear differential equations, interpreting limiting behaviour.

---

## 📙 Further Pure Mathematics 1 (FP1)

- [ ] **Chapter 1: Vectors (Advanced)** — Vector (cross) product $\mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}$, scalar triple product $\mathbf{a} \cdot (\mathbf{b} \times \mathbf{c})$, volume of parallelepiped and tetrahedron, vector equations of planes, perpendicular distance from point to plane.
- [ ] **Chapter 2: Conic Sections (Parabola & Rectangular Hyperbola)** — Parametric and Cartesian forms, equations of tangents and normals, focal properties, directrix.
- [ ] **Chapter 3: Properties of Conics & Loci** — Chords, midpoints, intersection of tangents, finding Cartesian equations of loci generated by moving parameters.
- [ ] **Chapter 4: Inequalities** — Algebraic inequalities with rational functions, modulus inequalities, graphical solutions.
- [ ] **Chapter 5: The $t$-Formulae** — Weierstrass substitutions $t = \tan(\frac{\theta}{2})$, solving trigonometric equations and evaluating definite/indefinite integrals.
- [ ] **Chapter 6: Taylor Series** — Taylor series expansions around $x = a$, series solutions to differential equations.
- [ ] **Chapter 7: Methods in Calculus (Advanced)** — Leibniz's theorem for $n$-th derivatives, L'Hôpital's Rule for indeterminate limits, reduction formulae for integrals.
- [ ] **Chapter 8: Numerical Methods** — Euler's method, Improved Euler's (Heun's) method, Runge-Kutta 2nd order or Taylor series methods for solving DEs numerically.

---

## 📗 Decision Mathematics 1 (D1)

- [ ] **Chapter 1: Algorithms & Complexity** — Big-$\mathcal{O}$ order of an algorithm, Bubble sort, Quick sort, Bin packing algorithms (First-fit, First-fit decreasing, Full-bin).
- [ ] **Chapter 2: Graphs and Networks** — Terminology (nodes, arcs, valency, bipartite, complete, tree, cycle), planar graphs, Euler's formula ($V + F - E = 2$), Hamiltonian vs Eulerian graphs.
- [ ] **Chapter 3: Algorithms on Networks** — Kruskal's algorithm, Prim's algorithm (graphic and tabular/matrix forms), Dijkstra's shortest path algorithm.
- [ ] **Chapter 4: Route Inspection (Chinese Postman Problem)** — Finding odd valency vertices, pairing odd vertices, finding minimum weight traversable route, edge cases with repeated edges.
- [ ] **Chapter 5: Critical Path Analysis (CPA)** — Activity networks, precedence tables, dummy activities (two reasons for dummies), forward and backward pass, early/late event times, total float, cascade/Gantt charts, resource scheduling and levelling.
- [ ] **Chapter 6: Linear Programming** — Formulating constraints and objective functions, drawing feasible regions, objective line (ruler) method, vertex testing method, finding optimal integer solutions.
